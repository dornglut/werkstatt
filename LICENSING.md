# Licensing Werkstatt

The current Werkstatt source is available under the **GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`)**. The complete public-license text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Commercial licensing

A separate commercial license may be available from copyright holder(s) that have sufficient rights to grant it. The commercial path is intended for organizations that require rights different from the AGPL-3.0-only terms, including proprietary deployment or integration where AGPL compliance is not suitable.

A commercial license is a separate agreement. Repository hosting, Dornglut membership, or this document does not by itself establish that any person or organization has sufficient rights to grant one. This repository does not define pricing, warranties, indemnities, service levels, support commitments, or other customer-specific commercial terms.

## Earlier revisions

This licensing change is prospective. Earlier Werkstatt revisions published under the MIT License retain the MIT rights already granted for those revisions. The current licensing policy does not revoke or reinterpret those earlier grants.

The migration issue and pull request retain the exact transition evidence for repository audit and due diligence.

## Third-party material

Dependencies and other third-party material remain governed by their own licenses. The Werkstatt repository license does not relabel or replace those terms.

## Contributions

The intended public/commercial licensing model requires sufficient relicensing rights over accepted copyright-bearing repository content. Until Dornglut adopts reviewed inbound contribution terms that preserve the commercial relicensing path, external pull requests that add tracked repository content are not accepted for Werkstatt, including code, documentation, tests, examples, build scripts, or assets.

Issue reports, design discussion, reviews, reproducible cases supplied through discussion, and other participation that does not add third-party repository content remain welcome.

See Dornglut Engineering ADR 0007 and the organization licensing standard for the governing repository policy.
